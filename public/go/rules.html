How to Play Go

Objective

Surround more territory than your opponent. The player who controls the most empty intersections at the end of the game wins.

Taking Turns

Black plays first. Players alternate placing one stone on any empty intersection. Once placed, stones do not move.

Liberties & Capture

Each stone (or connected group of same-color stones) must have at least one adjacent empty point — called a liberty. If your stones surround all of an opponent group's liberties, those stones are captured and removed from the board. Captured stones score one point each.

Ko

You cannot make a move that returns the board to its exact state from the previous turn. This prevents infinite loops of single-stone captures.

Passing & Game End

Either player may pass their turn instead of placing a stone. When both players pass consecutively, the game ends.

Scoring

Each player's score is their captures plus the empty intersections fully enclosed by their stones. The higher score wins.
